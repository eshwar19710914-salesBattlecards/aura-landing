The Problem Worth Solving

Good advisors lose deals
for fixable reasons

The gap between a great advisor and a struggling one isn't talent — it's access to the right information, at the right moment.

😰
Knowledge gaps kill trust in seconds
Advisors handle 14+ models, dozens of variants, and constantly changing specs. A hesitation on mileage figures or a wrong quote destroys the trust you spent months building.
A customer asks about Grand Vitara's strong hybrid range. The advisor pauses. Checks phone. Customer decides to "check online." They never come back.
😤
Competitor objections go unanswered
"Creta has better features." "Nexon has 5 stars." Without a ready counter, silence is read as confirmation. The deal walks out with the customer.
"I'll compare and come back" — said to an advisor who couldn't counter the Creta objection. Spoiler: they didn't come back.
📚
Training doesn't travel to the floor
Product training happens in quarterly sessions. Real selling happens every day. By the time a variant launches or a rival drops price, last week's training is already obsolete.
An advisor quoted incorrect variant pricing — 6 weeks after his last training session. The customer called it "unprofessional" and left — a ₹14L deal, gone.
