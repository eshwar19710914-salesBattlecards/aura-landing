# AURA AI Co-Pilot

## AI-powered Sales Enablement for Automotive Showrooms

AURA is an AI Co-Pilot for front-line automotive sales advisors, designed to provide instant product knowledge, competitor intelligence, sales conversation support and continuous training directly on the showroom floor.

The product concept focuses on helping sales teams respond faster and more confidently during customer conversations without requiring complex IT setup or a dedicated application download.

## 🎯 Product Vision

> **What if your newest advisor could sell like your best one?**

AURA aims to make product knowledge and sales expertise available to every advisor at the exact moment it is needed.

The platform brings together:

- Product and variant intelligence
- Real-time AI sales assistance
- Competitor battle cards
- Sales conversation guidance
- Gamified advisor training
- Mobile-first showroom experience

---

## 💼 Business Problem

Automotive showroom advisors need to handle customer questions and objections in real time.

However, they may face challenges such as:

- Difficulty recalling detailed product specifications
- Lack of immediate competitor information
- Inconsistent responses to customer objections
- Different levels of experience across advisors
- Limited time for structured product training
- Difficulty accessing information while actively engaging with customers

AURA is designed to address these challenges by putting relevant information and sales assistance directly into the advisor's workflow.

---

## 🚀 Key Capabilities

### 1. Real-Time AI Co-Pilot

AURA provides an AI-assisted interface where advisors can ask product or customer-objection questions during a live sales conversation.

The prototype demonstrates:

- Open-ended sales questions
- Pre-loaded scenario prompts
- Floor-ready responses
- Product and competitor responses
- Sales conversation guidance

The target experience is a response within a few seconds, enabling the advisor to continue the conversation without leaving the showroom floor.

---

### 2. Competitor Shield

AURA includes a **Competitor Shield** concept built around competitor battle cards.

The prototype highlights:

- 40+ competitor battle cards
- Side-by-side product comparisons
- Competitive talking points
- Floor-ready closing lines

The objective is to help advisors respond to competitor comparisons more consistently.

---

### 3. Full Lineup Intelligence

The platform concept provides access to:

- Vehicle specifications
- Pricing information
- Variant details
- Product information
- Persona-oriented sales pitches

The information is designed to be searchable quickly while the advisor is interacting with a customer.

---

### 4. Gamified Training Engine

AURA incorporates continuous learning into the sales workflow.

The prototype includes:

- Daily product quizzes
- Sales scenario simulations
- XP points
- Hub leaderboard
- Regular product-learning activities

This is intended to encourage advisors to continuously improve product and selling knowledge.

---

### 5. Mobile-First Experience

AURA is designed around the realities of the showroom floor.

The landing page describes:

- One-handed usage
- Mobile browser access
- PWA-style "Add to Home Screen"
- Offline capability after initial loading
- Android and iOS browser compatibility

The goal is to make the experience accessible without requiring a traditional application installation.
